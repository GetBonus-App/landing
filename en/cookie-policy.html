GetBonus attaches great importance to protecting your privacy and the security of your personal data. We comply with the requirements of Russian personal data law. When processing personal data we follow the principles set out in art. 5 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27.07.2006 “On Personal Data”. GetBonus processes the data described in this document (the “Policy”), collected while you use our websites: https://app.getbonus.red, https://t.me/getbonus, https://t.me/getbonusapp, https://t.me/getbonus/app (the “website”).

By continuing to use the website without changing your browser's cookie settings, you consent to the processing of cookies. You may also refuse the processing of cookies in accordance with sections 3 and 4 of this Policy. Please note that blocking cookies may affect the functionality of the site.

1. Terms

Cookies are small pieces of data that a website requests from the browser used on your computer or mobile device. Cookies contain information about your actions on the website and may also contain details of your hardware and the date and time of the session. We use cookies to improve the website and keep it working properly, and to improve the products and services of getbonus.red and our partners — in particular, by identifying your preferences so that we can give you targeted information about those products and services.

2. Cookies and automatic logging

Cookies are one of the technologies we use to collect information automatically and improve the quality of our content. Our web servers and security systems keep certain temporary technical data in log files. The cookies collected on our site are:

  • session cookies — these exist only in temporary memory for as long as the user is on a page of the website. Session cookies are usually deleted once you close the website window. They make it possible to remember the choices you made on the previous page so that you do not have to enter the same information again;
  • persistent cookies — cookies stored on your computer or mobile device that are not deleted when the browser is closed. Persistent cookies can keep user settings for a particular website so that those preferences can be used in future sessions. Such cookies make it possible to identify you as a unique user and, when you return to the website, help recall information about you and the actions you took earlier;
  • analytical/marketing cookies — these include information about how you use the website, for example which pages you visit and which links you follow. We use these cookies mainly to improve the website's features and your experience of using it. Sometimes such cookies also allow us to identify your preferences and give you useful targeted information about products and services that may interest you;
  • strictly necessary cookies — the minimum set of cookies required for the website to work correctly and without interruption.

3. Web analytics services

Our site uses the web analytics service “Yandex Metrica”, provided by Yandex LLC, 16 L. Tolstoy St., Moscow, 119021, Russia (“Yandex”). Yandex Metrica uses cookies to analyse user activity on our website.

The following types of personal data are processed: page URL, page referrer, page title, browser and its version, operating system and its version, device, screen height and width, availability of JavaScript, availability of cookies, time zone, browser language, screen colour depth, width and height of the browser's client area, visitors' gender and age, visitors' interests, geographic data (locality, region), records of the visitor's interactions with the site (for example, form submission, page scrolling, file download), page loading parameters, time on site, and browsing depth (number of pages viewed).

By using our website you agree to Yandex processing data about you in accordance with the procedure and purposes set out above. You can opt out of Yandex's use of cookies with the tool at https://yandex.ru/support/metrika/general/opt-out.html. However, this may affect how some features of the website work.

4. Changing your browser's cookie settings

Cookies are placed in the browser on your device — in most cases this happens automatically. You can refuse to have cookies stored and used on your device and/or delete cookies already stored, in your browser settings. If you refuse the processing of cookies, our website will use strictly necessary cookies only.
